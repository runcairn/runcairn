Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know Docker or Linux?

No. That's the whole point of Cairn. You sign up, you log in, you start using Paperless-ngx. We handle the entire technical stack.

Can I migrate my existing Paperless-ngx setup?

Yes — free white-glove migration is included with every plan. We move your documents, tags, correspondents, and document types. Your existing setup keeps running until you've confirmed everything is working on Cairn.

Where is my data stored?

US data centers (Ashburn, Virginia by default). Your documents are encrypted at rest and in transit. Daily encrypted backups to a separate location.

What if I want to leave?

No lock-in. Cairn runs the same Paperless-ngx that's open source. We give you a complete data export anytime — documents, tags, metadata, everything. You can self-host the same setup if you want.

How is Cairn different from PikaPods or Elestio?

Those are generic app hosts — they keep the server running but won't help you configure tags, fix OCR, or answer "how do I do X?" questions. Cairn provides real Paperless-specific application support from people who know the product.

What is "founding member" pricing?

The first 20 customers get 50% off their first 3 months. After that, regular pricing applies. Founding members also help shape the product roadmap and get direct access to me (Tez) for feedback.

Who is behind Cairn?

Tez Deloney — defense systems engineer, US military veteran (8 years, 2 deployments), real estate investor. I built Cairn because I needed Paperless-ngx for my own business and saw how hard it was for non-technical users to set up.
